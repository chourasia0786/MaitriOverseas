What we supply

Stainless steel, in the form your line needs

Every item below is available across all seven grades and can be slit, cut, circled or polished to your drawing before dispatch.

S.S. welded pipes & tubes

Manufactured in-house. Round, square and rectangular sections in mill and polished finishes.

Sheets & plates

2B, No.4 and mill finish. Shearing and cut-to-length to your panel sizes.

Coils

Full coils and slit coils. Held in stock for repeat schedules and blanket orders.

Round bars

Bright and black bar for machining, fasteners and shafting.

Circles

Blanked to diameter for utensils, pressure parts and deep-drawn components — less scrap than buying sheet and cutting it yourself.

Patta & patti

Slit strip in narrow widths, edge-conditioned and re-coiled to the weight your press or roll-former runs.

Grade reference

Pick a grade. See where it belongs.

Ferritic grades carry the exhaust and water-tank work; austenitic grades take the food, chemical and coastal jobs. Tap a number to read the short version.

409
Family
Ferritic · titanium stabilised
Chromium
10.5 – 11.7%
Known for
The most economical stainless. Good high-temperature oxidation resistance, weldable, magnetic.
Typically used in
Automotive exhaust systems, silencers, catalytic converter shells, agricultural equipment.
Worth knowing
Surface can bloom light rust in the open — specified where function, not appearance, is the point.
