Finish & source

The face of the steel, and the mill behind it

Two things buyers ask about after the grade: what the surface looks like, and whose steel it is. Both are your choice.

2B

Smooth mill finish

Cold rolled, annealed, pickled and skin-passed. Lightly reflective and even — the default for general fabrication and the most widely stocked finish we carry.

2D

Dull matte

Cold rolled, annealed and pickled without the final pass. Uniform and non-reflective, and it holds lubricant well — the one to pick for deep drawing.

No. 4

Brushed satin

A unidirectional brushed grain. The standard face for kitchen equipment, white goods and architectural panels where fingerprints have to disappear.

No. 8

Mirror

Progressively polished to a near-reflective surface. Decorative panels, trim, elevator interiors — anywhere the steel is meant to be looked at.

BA

Bright annealed

Annealed in a controlled atmosphere so it comes out bright without polishing. Clean, highly reflective, and free of the directional marks polishing leaves.
